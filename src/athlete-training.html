02 / A TYPICAL SESSION

How a session flows.

A typical athlete session moves from preparation into speed, jumping, and strength work. Exercise difficulty and the emphasis of the lift change with the athlete’s development and training phase.

  1. 01

    Prepare to move

    We start with foam rolling and soft-tissue work, followed by active stretches for the areas involved in the day’s workout.

  2. 02

    Dynamic warm-up

    A short dynamic warm-up gets athletes moving before the faster work begins.

  3. 03

    Speed and agility

    We work on acceleration, deceleration, changes of direction, and reactive work.

  4. 04

    Plyometrics and jump testing

    We incorporate extensive and intensive plyometrics, including single-leg, two-leg, and lateral movements.

  5. 05

    Strength training

    Our lift usually lasts 30–35 minutes, with the focus shaped by the athlete’s training phase, sport, and time of year.

  6. 06

    Conditioning or recovery

    When time allows, we add conditioning or recovery work based on the athlete’s training phase and whether they are in season or off season.

The sequence gives sessions structure without making every workout identical. Training phases change based on where athletes are in their development.

05 / BEFORE YOU START

A few common questions.

Who is athlete training for?+

WSP works with middle school, high school, college, and professional athletes. If you are unsure which session fits your athlete, contact us before booking.

Is this sport practice?+

This program develops physical qualities that support your sport. It complements the skills and practice you do with your team or sport coach.

Can I train during my season?+

Talk with Ben about your practice and competition schedule so you can discuss how training could fit alongside your sport.

How often should I attend?+

Memberships offer one to four sessions per week. Your starting point should fit your goals and schedule; contact WSP for help choosing.
